Driver Compensation and Benefits

Compensation

  • Drivers are generously compensated and have opportunities to increase earnings through work incentive programs.
  • Paid vacation: 1st year, 1 week; 2nd through end of 4th year, 2 weeks; 5th year, 3 weeks.
  • Paid holidays.
  • Cash safety incentive program.
  • Cash weekend work incentive program.

Benefits

  • Company-paid full medical, dental and life insurance for the employee.
  • 50% of benefit costs paid for dependents.
  • 401(k) full matching up to 3%.
  • Paid uniform service.
  • Company-paid fitness physicals.

Requirements

  • Valid Class A license.
  • Clean 10-year H6 DMV printout.
  • Two years of driving experience required. Tank experience preferred.
  • Doubles/Triples, Hazardous Material and Tank Vehicle endorsements.
  • Current and valid medical card.
