Why 24-Hour Dispatch Matters in Petroleum Transportation

Petroleum transportation does not always fit a normal office schedule. Direct access to dispatch gives customers a clear path when timing changes or a new transportation need comes up.

Transportation Schedules Can Change

Pickup timing, customer priorities and delivery requirements can change after normal office hours. A customer should know who to call when that happens. Petrol Transport maintains a 24-hour on-call dispatch center in Bakersfield for that reason.

The value of around-the-clock dispatch is simple: customers do not have to wait until the next business day to communicate an active transportation need.

Good Dispatch Starts With Useful Information

When contacting a carrier, have the basic job details ready. Useful information includes the customer name, service area, requested timing, pickup and delivery information if known, and the best callback number.

For urgent requests, a phone call is usually the fastest path. Online forms are useful for work that can be reviewed and followed up by the office.

One Contact Path Across California Service Areas

Petrol Transport does not create separate dispatch offices for every service-area page on this website. Bakersfield remains the company’s listed office and dispatch location, including for service in Santa Maria, Los Angeles and Long Beach.
