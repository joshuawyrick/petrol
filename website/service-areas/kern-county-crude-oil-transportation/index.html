Kern County Is Home Territory

Petrol Transport’s office and dispatch operation are in Bakersfield. For Kern County customers, that means transportation requests are handled from the company’s home market rather than routed through a distant office.

Transportation Built Around Customer Requirements

Crude oil transportation work varies by customer and location. Pickup timing, delivery requirements and operating conditions can all affect the job. Petrol Transport focuses on clear communication and coordination rather than forcing every customer into the same process.

The company has served California since 1979 and has long emphasized driver safety, reliability, efficiency and professional training.
