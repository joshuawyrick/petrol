From One Truck to One of California's Most Respected Crude Oil Carriers

We started with a single truck, a handshake, and a commitment to do the job right. More than four decades later, Petrol Transport Inc. remains a family-owned, Bakersfield-based operation hauling crude oil across California.

How It All Started

In 1979, Ted "Brownie" Barnard, Jr. and his college classmate Dennis Burtch found themselves on the same side of a problem. Burtch had recently taken over his father's company, Burtch Trucking, which was hauling oil at a lease in the Poso Creek area. His crews were falling behind, and he was concerned he would lose the haul.

Burtch reached out to Brownie, who was working as a ranch foreman for Joughin Ranch after graduating from Cal Poly, and asked if he would help out by observing the lease for a week to determine the problem and find a solution. After a few days, Brownie recognized that each new crew was repeating the same mistakes as the one before. His suggestion: put a dedicated crew on the haul so they could learn from their own errors.

Burtch could not spare a dedicated crew, so he asked Brownie to purchase his own truck and operate under Burtch Trucking. Brownie agreed, and Flying B Trucking was born from that one truck.

The First Haul

Brownie's first load was one shipment of fuel oil from Bakersfield to Long Beach. He was driving an older "needle nose" Peterbilt truck with two sticks — a main gear box with five gears and an auxiliary gear box with an additional four gears.

The haul was challenging from the start. Brownie had to cross the Grapevine — a treacherous 200-mile stretch of Interstate 5 from Los Angeles to Kern County — during a snowstorm. When he arrived at his destination, the fuel oil had become cold and viscous, taking twelve hours to offload. By the time he was back on the road, the Grapevine was closed due to the storm, and he had to return to Bakersfield via the coastal route.

That initial load took twenty-four hours to complete. When Brownie arrived back at the yard, the vice president of Burtch Trucking signed off on his training, commenting that in a twenty-four hour period he had experienced more than he would have in a year of training.

Building the Company

For the next three years, Brownie did double duty — driving his Flying B truck at night and working as a ranch foreman during the day. The grueling schedule meant plenty of twenty-hour days.

In 1982, Brownie learned that Burtch was interested in selling his trucking company. Two of Burtch's vice presidents, as well as Brownie, were interested in purchasing it. A short time later, during a fishing trip to Canada, Brownie saw his opportunity. He gave the fishing guide a generous gratuity to make sure he and Burtch ended up on the same boat — and made his pitch.

The result was a handshake deal that allowed Brownie to acquire the company with a down payment and monthly payments over the next five years. The purchase increased Flying B's fleet to sixteen trucks, and the company name was changed to Petrol Transport.

The Next Generation

Under Brownie, Petrol Transport grew and became very successful — so much so that in 2009 another trucking company expressed interest in buying the firm. When Brownie's son Zach Barnard, who was working for an inspection company in New Mexico after his discharge from the Navy, heard of the possible sale, he returned home.

"I couldn't imagine Petrol Transport being sold and so I made the decision to come home and work in the trucking business."— Zach Barnard

Zach became a partner in 2010. Since that time, Petrol Transport has continued to grow and increase its customer base, and is now one of the largest and most highly regarded crude trucking companies in the Southern San Joaquin and Santa Maria Valley regions.

Our Guiding Principle

Zach continues to operate Petrol Transport by the principles under which his father started the company more than four decades ago. Brownie always said:

"I never wanted to be the biggest, I only wanted to be the best."— Ted "Brownie" Barnard, Jr.

"We have lived up to that principle, just ask our customers, and we will continue to do so for the next forty years," says Zach.

Core values

Driver Safety. Reliability. Efficiency.

These three principles have guided us since the day Brownie completed that first twenty-four-hour haul.

Safety

Every employee is thoroughly trained on hazardous material handling, FMCSA rules, DOT regulations, and job-specific safety duties. We are dedicated to protecting our people, our customers, and the environment.

Reliability

Our 24-hour dispatch center means you can reach a real person at any hour. Whether it is a scheduled haul, a last-minute change, or an urgent need, we are here to serve.

Efficiency

We keep a lean operation through smart logistics and decades of experience. The result: competitive rates and transportation that moves without unnecessary delay.
