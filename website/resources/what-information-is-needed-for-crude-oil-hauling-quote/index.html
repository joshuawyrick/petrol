What Information Is Needed for a Crude Oil Hauling Quote?

A thorough estimate request helps dispatch evaluate your transportation needs quickly and accurately. Here is what to prepare before you call or submit a request.

Request EstimateCall 661-393-6514

Pickup Location and Lease Information

Provide the lease name or well site description, along with GPS coordinates or a street address. The more specific you can be, the faster dispatch can evaluate access routes and drive times. If the location has restricted access, narrow roads, or requires specific entry procedures, include those details.

Delivery Destination

Where does the crude oil need to go? Identify the refinery, terminal, pipeline inlet, or rail facility. The destination affects route planning, drive time, and scheduling.

Estimated Volume and Frequency

Share your estimated barrels per day and whether the work is a one-time request or recurring transportation. If recurring, describe the expected frequency: daily, several times per week, weekly, or another schedule. This helps dispatch assess whether the operation can support your needs.

API Gravity

API gravity describes the density of the crude oil and can affect handling and transportation planning. Provide it when available, but it is not required to start the conversation.

Timing and Start Date

When do you need transportation to begin? If the start date is flexible, say so. If there is a deadline, communicate it upfront so dispatch can confirm availability.

Site Access and Operating Requirements

Note any access restrictions, truck size limitations, hours when pickups are allowed, customer-specific safety requirements, or other operating conditions. This information helps prevent delays after transportation has started.

Your Contact Information

Provide your name, company, phone number, and email so dispatch can follow up with questions and a quote. The more complete your initial request, the faster the office can respond.
