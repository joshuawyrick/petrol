Safety

Safety at Petrol Transport

Petrol Transport is dedicated to protecting employee health and safety and taking appropriate steps to help protect the environment during petroleum transportation work.

Request TransportationCall 661-393-6514

Training for the Work Employees Are Assigned

Petrol Transport’s current public information states that employees are thoroughly trained on hazardous material requirements, Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration rules, Department of Transportation regulations and safety duties specific to the work they are assigned.

Safety is not presented as a separate marketing program. It is part of the way the company describes driver qualification, employee responsibility and petroleum transportation operations.

Driver Safety, Reliability and Efficiency

The existing Petrol Transport website highlights driver safety, reliability and efficiency as three core operating themes. The redesigned site keeps those ideas together because they affect the same customer outcome: transportation that is coordinated carefully and supported by trained people.

Customers with specific safety or compliance questions should contact Petrol Transport directly so the office can provide information that applies to the requested work.
