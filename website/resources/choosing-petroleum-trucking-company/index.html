Choosing a carrier

What to Look for in a Petroleum Trucking Company

The right transportation relationship depends on more than a rate quote. Customers should be able to understand how to reach dispatch, where the carrier serves and how the company approaches safety and communication.

Request EstimateCall 661-393-6514

Start With Availability and Communication

Ask how transportation requests are handled outside normal office hours. For work that can change quickly, knowing who answers the phone is often more useful than a long list of marketing claims.

Petrol Transport lists 24/7 operating hours and maintains its dispatch operation in Bakersfield.

Confirm the Service Area

A carrier should clearly state where it serves without implying it has offices that do not exist. Petrol Transport is based in Bakersfield and serves California markets that include Kern County, Santa Maria, Los Angeles and Long Beach.

For customers, this makes it easier to understand who is coordinating the work and where to direct questions.

Ask About Training and Safety Expectations

Petrol Transport’s current public information states that employees are trained on hazardous material requirements, Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration rules, Department of Transportation regulations and job-specific safety duties.

Customers with detailed compliance requirements should discuss them directly with the carrier before the work begins.

Compare the Whole Service Relationship

Rate matters, but so do responsiveness, reliability and the ability to communicate throughout the job. Petrol Transport has publicly emphasized competitive pricing, efficient operations, driver safety and reliability for years.
