Sharing the Heritage

The current Petrol Transport website describes the company with a simple line: “Sharing the Heritage, Est. 1979.” That history is worth keeping because it tells customers something more useful than a generic marketing statement. Petrol Transport has been part of California petroleum transportation for decades.

The business is based in Bakersfield and maintains 24-hour dispatch. Its public message has consistently focused on availability, reliable service, efficient operations, professional training and safety.

A Direct Relationship With Customers

Petrol Transport states that it values both existing and new customer relationships. The redesigned website is organized around that same idea. Customers should be able to quickly understand what the company does, where it serves and how to reach dispatch.

There is no need to make the business sound more complicated than it is. Petrol Transport moves crude oil and petroleum for California customers and keeps a direct line open to the people coordinating the work.

Core operating themes

Driver Safety. Reliability. Efficiency.

These three ideas appear on the current Petrol Transport site and remain part of the new company story.

Safety

Employees are trained on hazardous material requirements, FMCSA rules, DOT regulations and job-specific safety duties. Protecting employees and the environment is part of every job.

Reliability

24-hour dispatch means customers can reach Petrol Transport at any hour. Scheduled work, changing needs and time-sensitive requests are all handled through the Bakersfield operation.

Efficiency

Decades of California petroleum transportation experience translate to coordinated operations, direct customer communication and transportation that is dispatched without unnecessary delay.
