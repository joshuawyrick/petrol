Our commitment

Safety Built Into Every Mile

At Petrol Transport, we are dedicated to protecting our employees, our customers, and the environment. Safety is not just a policy — it is how we operate every single day.

Our Safety Culture

We believe every accident is preventable. That commitment starts with thorough training before any driver gets behind the wheel and continues with ongoing education, regular safety meetings, and a culture where every team member is empowered to stop work if conditions are unsafe.

Our drivers handle hazardous materials across some of California’s most demanding terrain. We take that responsibility seriously, and we invest in the people and practices needed to do it safely.

Professionally Trained Drivers

Every employee is thoroughly trained on all Hazardous Material, Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration, and Department of Transportation rules and regulations, as well as safety-specific duties for the job they will be assigned.

We do not send a driver on the road until they are fully prepared. Our training covers the unique hazards associated with crude oil transportation, defensive driving techniques, emergency response procedures, and environmental protection protocols.

Our safety pillars

How We Protect People and the Environment

Hazardous Material Training

Every driver is trained on proper hazmat handling, spill prevention, and emergency response. We follow strict protocols to protect both our team and the communities we drive through.

FMCSA & DOT Compliance

We maintain full compliance with Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration and Department of Transportation regulations. Our safety record reflects our commitment to meeting and exceeding these standards.

Job-Specific Safety

Crude oil transportation presents unique challenges. We train our drivers on the specific hazards of each assignment, from well site pickups to refinery deliveries across California's varied terrain.

Environmental Responsibility

We take every precaution to prevent spills and protect the environment. Our equipment is maintained to the highest standards, and our drivers are trained to recognize and respond to environmental risks immediately.
